human-anatomy-correctives.glb: experimental derivative of human-anatomy-v2.glb
with elbowFlex_L/R and kneeFlex_L/R POSITION/NORMAL targets computed offline
using Blender 5.2.2 Preserve Volume (dual quaternion deformation) and inverse LBS.
This restores sampled deformation volume; it is not an anatomical muscle sculpt.
Reproduce with node scripts/blender/build_correctives.mjs <Blender executable>.
Calibration and sparse deltas: volume-correctives.json.

Not approved for production: knees remain angular, and general activation needs
artistic validation and memory profiling. Production human.glb stays unchanged.
